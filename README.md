## 🎮 About the Game

ZENONIA 1 is a single-player action RPG developed by GAMEVIL and first released on May 24, 2009. The PC restoration launched on August 31, 2026, with development credited to Com2uS Holdings and CFK. Players control Regret through a story-driven fantasy adventure featuring real-time combat, character classes, quests, equipment, skills, and moral choices.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **ZENONIA 1**. This trainer provides optional gameplay assistance for ZENONIA 1 by modifying common single-player resources and progression systems, including health, SP, gold, experience, attributes, skill points, hunger, equipment durability, and combat damage.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to replay ZENONIA 1 with reduced grinding, easier combat, faster character development, or more freedom to experiment with classes, equipment, and skills.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Health** — Prevents Regret's health from reaching zero during normal gameplay.
- **Multiply Experience** — Increases experience gained from defeating enemies and completing quests.
- **No Hunger** — Stops the hunger meter from decreasing, reducing the need to consume food while exploring.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited SP** — Keeps skill points available for repeated use of class abilities.
- **One-Hit Kills** — Greatly increases outgoing damage against regular enemies.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Gold** — Provides a large or locked gold balance for purchasing equipment, supplies, repairs, and other items.
- **Unlimited Equipment Durability** — Prevents equipped weapons and armor from losing durability and reduces the need for repairs.

### 📌 Progression

- **Add Attribute Points** — Allows additional points to be assigned to Regret's character attributes after leveling.
- **Add Skill Points** — Provides extra points for unlocking and improving active and passive class skills.

### 🌍 World

- **Freeze Day-Night Cycle** — Pauses the game's day-night clock at its current time.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Explore towns, fields, caves, and other areas in real time. Use the trainer's hunger option to travel and search for quests without regularly stopping to consume food. The day-night freeze option is useful when a quest or activity depends on the current time.

### Tools & Inventory

ZENONIA 1 uses equipment, consumable items, gold, item requirements, durability, and inventory management. Unlimited gold helps with purchases and repairs, while unlimited durability keeps equipped weapons and armor usable. Avoid changing item values while buying, selling, combining, or equipping gear.

### World & Environment

The campaign follows Regret through a large fantasy world containing towns, NPCs, story missions, side quests, dungeons, enemies, and moral choices. Experience multiplication and attribute points can shorten repeated combat, while freezing the day-night cycle can make time-sensitive exploration more convenient.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat takes place in real time with normal attacks, class-specific skills, SP consumption, equipment bonuses, and healing items. Activate unlimited health for survivability, unlimited SP for unrestricted skill use, or one-hit kills for faster battles. Disable damage-related options before major story encounters if you want to preserve the intended challenge.
